# Jazzster: a home-made jazz version of Hitster

There are two pages:

- **index.html** is the player. Scanning a card opens it, and it plays the song without showing the title.
- **cards.html** is the card maker. You enter songs and print double-sided cards from it.

## 1. Put it online for free (GitHub Pages, about 10 minutes)

1. Create a free account at github.com.
2. Click **+** at the top right, choose **New repository**, name it `jazzster`, set it to **Public**, and click **Create repository**.
3. Click **uploading an existing file** and drag in all the files from this folder: `index.html`, `cards.html`, `qrcode.js` and this README. Click **Commit changes**.
4. Go to **Settings → Pages**. Under "Branch", choose `main` and `/ (root)`, then click **Save**.
5. After a minute or two the site is live at `https://YOUR-NAME.github.io/jazzster/`.

Note: anyone who knows that address can open the site. It only plays songs and doesn't show anything private.

## 2. Make the cards

1. Open `https://YOUR-NAME.github.io/jazzster/cards.html` on a computer.
2. The 10 example songs are already filled in. Add a Spotify link for each one: in Spotify, right-click the song, choose **Share → Copy Song Link**, and paste it. Pick the version from the original album, not a "Best of".
   Quicker: put the songs in a Spotify playlist in the same order, select them all in the desktop app (Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+C) and paste them into "Fill in many links at once".
3. Click **▶ Test** on a row to check that the song plays.
4. Click **Print cards**. Print double-sided on A4 at 100% scale, on thick paper (200 gsm or more), and cut along the lines.

The song list is saved in that browser. Use "Copy list" to keep a backup.

## 3. Play

Scan a card with your normal phone camera and tap the link. Press the big play button.

- If the button does nothing, tap **No sound?** and press the round button inside the blurred player.
- Songs may play as a 30-second preview unless you're signed in to Spotify in that phone's browser (open.spotify.com). For guessing a year, 30 seconds is usually enough.

Rules: use the normal Hitster rules. The year on each card is the **recording year**.
