Jazzster card maker

Each card has a QR code on the front and the artist, title and recording year on the back. Scanning the code opens the Jazzster player, which plays the song without showing its title.

1. Settings

Where your index.html is hosted. Filled in automatically.

2. Songs

Spotify linkArtistTitleYear

Fill in many links at once

In the Spotify desktop app, open your playlist, click a song, press Ctrl+A (Cmd+A on Mac) and then Ctrl+C. Paste here. The links fill rows 1, 2, 3… in playlist order, and new rows are added if needed.

Save or load the list as text

One song per line: link, artist, title, year, separated by tabs. You can paste from a spreadsheet, and copy it back out to keep a backup. The list is also remembered in this browser.

3. Print

  1. Print double-sided on A4, at 100% scale (turn off "fit to page"). Thick paper, 200 gsm or more, works best.
  2. Each front page is followed by its back page. Check one sheet first, because printers can shift the back by a millimetre or two.
  3. Cut along the thin lines.
